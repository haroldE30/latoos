Our Story

Built by players,
for our local community.

LATOOS started the way most good ideas do — three friends on a court, wishing the gear was better without costing more.

We picked up pickleball together about three months ago. Like a lot of new players, we quickly fell in love with the game — and just as quickly got frustrated with our options. The paddles that actually performed well were priced for tournament pros, and the affordable ones felt like an afterthought.

So we decided to build the paddle we wished we could buy. Not a gimmick, not a reskin of someone else's mold — real materials, real engineering, priced for the local community we actually play in.

LATOOS is still small. We're players first, and that's exactly the point — every decision we make about our paddles starts with the question, "would we want to play with this ourselves?"
